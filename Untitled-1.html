Frequently Addressed Queries

Essential clarity concerning cadastral certification, smart infrastructure, and purchase flexibility.

Every residence in The Denim Horizon Precinct holds pre-split freehold ownership (Sertifikat Hak Milik / SHM) and an approved building consent (Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung / PBG). Upon down payment completion and KPR approval, signing of the Deed of Sale and Purchase (AJB) proceeds directly before an authorized notary without delay.

Standard delivery includes a Zigbee central hub, biometric smart digital door lock (passcode, fingerprint, RF card, physical fail-safe key), two outdoor PTZ infrared security cameras, smart lighting switches in the living salon, and an integrated real-time household electrical energy consumption monitor. All electronic subsystems carry a full 2-year developer replacement warranty.

Yes. We offer an interest-free developer installment program for down payments (DP dicicil hingga 12x tanpa bunga). The initial booking fee of IDR 25 Million directly offsets the first down payment tranche, with the remaining equity split equally across 12 consecutive calendar months prior to bank disbursement.

The comprehensive estate management fee (Iuran Pengelolaan Lingkungan / IPL) is fixed at IDR 8.500 per square meter of land area per month. This covers 24/7 manned security guards, automated RFID gate maintenance, communal linear park landscaping, solar lap pool water treatment, glasshouse clubhouse utilities, and daily precinct rubbish collection.
